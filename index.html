Count the drawer bill by bill. See what Square expected only after.

A blind end-of-day cash close for Square sellers. Staff enter how many of each bill and coin they have; DrawerCount totals it, then reveals the expected cash, the over/short and what makes up the expected amount. It also matches card sales minus fees to the bank deposit.

This is a demo. It does not connect to Square and nothing you type leaves this page. The live version connects to your Square account (read-only) and pulls the shift's expected cash, paid-ins/outs and refunds automatically. All numbers marked SAMPLE are made up for illustration.

Why this exists

Square sellers have asked for denomination counting since 2022. From the Square Community feature request (Sep 2026):

“Built-in denomination counter. Let staff enter counts by bill and coin (20 × $1, 6 × $5, etc.) and have Square total it automatically.”Square seller, Square Community, Sep 2026
“We ultimately stopped having our staff count the drawer when we switched from toast to square because this was missing.”Square seller, Square Community, Sep 2026
